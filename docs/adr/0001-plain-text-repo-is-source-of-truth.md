# Plain-text repo is the source of truth for conclusions and research

Conclusions (the tree) and research both live as plain-text files in git, not in genealogy software such as Ancestry or Reunion. Agents and scripts must be able to read and reason over conclusions alongside the evidence behind them, and the whole body of work must survive without any subscription or proprietary program: one script should turn it into an Archive that can be handed to family or a library. Ancestry becomes a place to search for records and get hints; what it turns up enters the repo as Sources and Citations, and nothing syncs back. Reunion and similar programs are optional viewers fed by a GEDCOM export.

## Consequences

We give up the data-entry screens and automatic record hints of genealogy software, and must replace them with our own tools or agent workflows.
