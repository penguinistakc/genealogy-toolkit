# Genealogy Toolkit

Personal genealogical research, conducted and documented according to the Genealogical Proof Standard.

## Language

### Method

**Genealogical Proof Standard**:
The five-part standard a conclusion must meet to be considered proved: reasonably exhaustive research, complete citations, analysis and correlation of evidence, resolution of conflicting evidence, and a written conclusion.
_Avoid_: GPS standard, proof method

**Research Question**:
A focused question about a specific identity, relationship, or event that a piece of research sets out to answer.
_Avoid_: Goal, objective, problem

**Proof Argument**:
A written conclusion explaining how the evidence answers a Research Question, including how conflicts were resolved.
_Avoid_: Write-up, report, case

### Evidence analysis

**Source**:
A container of information — a document, artifact, or publication — classified as original, derivative, or authored.
_Avoid_: Record, document (as the general term)

**Information**:
The content of a Source, classified as primary, secondary, or undetermined according to the informant's knowledge of what it reports.
_Avoid_: Data, facts

**Evidence**:
Information interpreted as relevant to a specific Research Question, classified as direct, indirect, or negative.
_Avoid_: Proof, facts

**Citation**:
A reference that identifies a Source, and where within it the Information was found, precisely enough for someone else to find it again.
_Avoid_: Reference, footnote

**Media**:
A stored digital file belonging to a Source, either a copy of it (a scan or photo) or the Source itself (an interview recording).
_Avoid_: Artifact, source image, evidence file, attachment

**Artifact**:
A physical object that is itself a Source, such as a gravestone, family Bible, or sampler.
_Avoid_: Object, item

**Repository**:
The institution or collection that holds a Source, such as an archive, library, courthouse, or a family's own papers.
_Avoid_: Archive (for the institution), repo

**Persona**:
A person exactly as a single Source describes them, errors included, recorded before any judgment about who they really were; disagreements with other Sources are resolved in Conclusions, never by changing the Persona.
_Avoid_: Mention, record person, entry

### Conclusions

**Conclusion**:
An assertion about a Person's identity, life events, or relationships, reached by analysing Evidence — including which Personas refer to the same Person. Together, Conclusions make up the tree.
_Avoid_: Fact, tree data

**Person**:
A real individual, as established by Conclusions that identify one or more Personas as referring to them.
_Avoid_: Individual, profile, ancestor (as the general term)

**Confidence**:
How well supported a Conclusion is: **Proved** (meets the Genealogical Proof Standard, with a Proof Argument), **Probable** (evidence leans this way, but research or conflict resolution is incomplete), **Possible** (plausible but weakly supported), or **Hypothesis** (a working guess not yet researched).
_Avoid_: Certainty, status, quality

**Proposal**:
A suggested change to the research, such as a Persona abstraction, a Persona match, or a draft Proof Argument, made by an agent and awaiting the researcher's review; only the researcher sets or raises Confidence.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, hint

### Sharing

**Research Vault**:
The private body of research (Sources, Personas, Conclusions, Proof Arguments, and their Media) kept apart from the public toolkit.
_Avoid_: Research repo, database, tree

**Archive**:
A self-contained bundle of the research (a browsable offline website, a printable PDF, a GEDCOM file, and the original files) that can be read without any subscription or genealogy software.
_Avoid_: Backup, dump

**Possibly Living**:
Describes a person born within the last 100 years with no recorded death; treated as living, and left out of anything shared outside the research by default.
_Avoid_: Recent, private person
